Biography

This is my story, my story? My life? I have nothing to say, read it yourself:

  1. [01]//childhood.log

    Hello! I'm a young Iranian

    Yeah, I'm Iranian. Since childhood I grew up surrounded by phones, the internet, and especially operating systems. But I never really pursued them.

  2. [02]//nickname.log

    The "Zombie" nickname

    From first to sixth grade I didn't have many friends and always kept to myself. For some reason others called me strange and gave me the nickname "Zombie" — because I was different, not in character, but physically. I had allergies; four out of seven days a week I had a runny nose.

    Still, I had friends who had my back, but I understood that everyone has their own problems.

  3. [03]//minecraft.exe

    Discovering Minecraft

    At 13 I discovered a game called Minecraft. It pulled me into a world of creativity. Unlike other games, I learned to use commands in this one — with command blocks. I found it fascinating!

  4. [04]//covid.era

    COVID and 8th grade

    When 8th grade started, COVID hit and I couldn't really do anything that period. The reason was me, honestly — I was lazy.

  5. [05]//piano.song

    Piano and concerts

    In 9th grade I decided to branch out beyond my usual stuff. Since I was introverted but also craved attention, I decided to start music. Playing piano and three concerts in 3 years — one with 30 people, then 100, then around 300.

  6. [06]//command_blocks.sh

    Why Minecraft was so appealing

    Because I loved showing people what I built. I had learned 70% of the game's commands! I wanted to make maps — Bed Wars, Sky Wars, TNT Run.

  7. [07]//network/

    Networking and software

    That's how my field of study became networking and software. When I entered my program, I also started learning English — I figured for someone in my field, language is essential!

  8. [08]//video_edit.mp4

    Burnt out on programming

    During my studies, since it was early days with programming, I got burnt out — it was hard. I went toward video editing; Instagram-style edits and such. I was decent at it, but since I didn't have a proper system and worked on my phone, it fell a bit short of professional.

  9. [09]//my_pc.desktop

    Finally got a PC

    When I finally got a system, I decided to continue video editing, but the software felt confusing because I'd spent years working on a phone.

  10. [10]//linux/test

    Linux and its basics

    Then I decided to try Linux and see how it is. I tested various distros and learned the basics — honestly, it was enjoyable.

  11. [11]//friends.mp4

    My friends, the most important thing

    I also made videos with my friends, who matter more to me than anything — both virtual and close ones. They really matter to me. Maybe you'll be the next one?

  12. [12]//python.learning

    Now: Python

    And then I decided to start programming from scratch, and currently I'm learning Python!
